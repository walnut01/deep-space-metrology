02. Mathematical Formulations & Physics Matrix

Complete analytical formulations governing body wave velocity, cavity resonance, impulse transfer, signal correlation, spectral density, and phonon preservation.

EQUATION 01 — SEISMIC WAVE VELOCITY

Compressional Wave Speed (vp)

Calculates the primary P-wave velocity in a solid rock matrix based on Bulk Modulus K, Shear Modulus μ, and volumetric mass density ρ.

vp = √ [ ( K + (4/3)μ ) / ρ ]
For typical silicate rock (ρ = 2700 kg/m³, K = 25 GPa, μ = 15 GPa), vp evaluates precisely to 4082.5 m/s.
EQUATION 02 — CAVITY RESONANCE

Fundamental Mode Frequency (f0)

Determines the intrinsic natural acoustic vibration frequency of an asteroid body of characteristic length L following impulse impact.

f0 ≈ vp / ( 2 · L )
For Asteroid 99942 Apophis (L = 340 m, vp = 4082.5 m/s), the fundamental structural mode computes to f0 ≈ 6.00 Hz.
EQUATION 03 — IMPULSE ENHANCEMENT

Momentum Factor (β)

Quantifies total momentum transfer to the target body by combining direct spacecraft impact momentum with crater ejecta recoil momentum.

β = 1 + ( p_ejecta / p_impactor )
Validated by DART at Dimorphos (β ≈ 2.2 to 3.6), confirming significant ejecta plume impulse multiplication.
EQUATION 04 — TELEMETRY RECOVERY

Cross-Correlation Function Rxy(τ)

Extracts ultra-weak returned signals y(t) from deep-space noise by correlation with the transmitted pulse template x(t).

Rxy(τ) = ∫ x(t) · y(t + τ) dt
Determines absolute round-trip delay time τ_echo = argmax |Rxy(τ)| even under negative SNR conditions (-28 dB).
EQUATION 05 — SPECTRAL ESTIMATION

Welch Power Density Pxx(f)

Converts time-series telemetry x[n] into frequency domain power spectrum using Hann windowing w[n] to reduce spectral leakage.

Pxx(f) = (1 / MU) · | Σ x[n] w[n] e^(-j2πfn) |²
Isolates discrete resonance peaks at 6.00 Hz (fundamental), 28.32 Hz (kinetic impact), and 43.95 Hz (upper harmonic).
EQUATION 06 — CRYOGENIC PHONONS

Quality Factor (Q) in Oort Silicon

Models the extreme elevation of lattice phonon lifetime in silicon at deep space cryogenic temperatures (10 K - 30 K).

Q ∝ 1 / ( α_viscous · Temp^4 )
As Temp → 10 K, thermal phonon dissipation approaches zero, resulting in mechanical Q > 10^5 (un-damped propagation).
